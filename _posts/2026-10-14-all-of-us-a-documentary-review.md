I saw on Facebook that there is a new documentary about AAC called "All Of Us". I thought to myself hey that sounds like a great topic for a blog. So I got in my bed to watch the documentary on Amazon Prime or Apple TV. It follows a grade school called Ruby Bridges in Washington state on their journey to educate all students in the regular education classrooms.

When they opened up in 2020 the principals and staff believed and believed in inclusive education. That is so important here. If the principals don't believe in this, it is likely that it won't happen. The documentary mostly takes place in the early grades kindergarten to third grade. One powerful line from the kindergarten teacher was "my own children are in middle school and they don't get to see their friends much. Their friends were over to my house for birthday parties and play dates, and now they don't see their friends at school". For me it felt like I was watching my friend's mother when she said that, because my friend's mother was one of the aides at grade school. I thought she was so right, sadly.

Another powerful thing that happened in the documentary was children were telling what they want to be. One child asked her brother who is nonverbal {I am guessing here because he couldn't speak but they didn't really say} she said this sentence. I wonder what you want to be. Man that hit home for me, because it would have been my older brother who would have said that sentence 20 years ago. If we don't value every single child who is nonverbal then we will be missing out on the child's ability.

When watching this documentary, I think of the many people that I know and worked with that weren't in an inclusive setting because of their behavior challenges or/and cognitive abilities. Hey, guess what this documentary showed what to do when a student has behavioral challenges in the regular education classrooms. And I think this was an important thing to show, because I have heard from teachers and families that because they have behaviors and use a communication device, they need to be in the special education area. Like I wrote in the letter to the secretary of education, that is true for some of the students, but I believe that some of these students may benefit from the regular education system. Like my friends and I did.

I hope you can watch All Of Us and they have more information available on their website. https://allofus-themovie.com/
